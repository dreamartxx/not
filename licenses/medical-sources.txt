ECG teaching summaries and images

The ECG adaptations in assets/firstaid-data.js credit Life in the Fast Lane
(LITFL), ECG Library, https://litfl.com/ecg-library/basics/.
Each topic includes its exact article URL. The image URL is taken from that
article; images are loaded from LITFL and are not republished as local copies.

LITFL's stated license: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
Changes: original Turkish educational summaries, reordered learning steps,
and links to the source examples. These adaptations carry the same license.
The application is a private personal workspace, not a commercial ECG library.

Original SVG teaching diagrams in assets/knowledge-visuals.js are schematic,
not patient records. They are offered under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as well.

Other medical topics are short original summaries with per-topic links to
AHA 2025 adult algorithms, Resuscitation Council UK 2025, NHS and St John
Ambulance. The app does not reproduce their full documents or imply endorsement.
Source review date: 7 November 2026. Not an official Turkish translation.

Legislation

assets/isg-data.js contains public-domain legislation transcriptions obtained
from mevzuat.wiki. It excludes site editorial text. The law snapshot is dated
25 July 2026 and the seven regulation snapshots 2 August 2026, as declared by
the source. These are archival dates, not effective dates or verification of
current force. Official binding texts must be checked via mevzuat.gov.tr and
Resmi Gazete. Per-document source and snapshot metadata is retained in the UI.

PubChem

Chemical data is fetched dynamically from NCBI PubChem PUG-REST / PUG-View.
Each annotation retains its contributing source, citation, license notice and
license URL when provided. No blanket license is asserted over third-party
annotations. The sixteen headings are an SDS-shaped research view, not an SDS
issued by a manufacturer or an assertion of Turkish regulatory compliance.
